FibroFog is a fibromyalgia tracker built for the days when typing is too much. Tap how bad it is, or just talk. Weather, sleep, and steps log themselves. Over time it shows you your baseline, what tends to come before a flare, and turns it all into a one-page summary your clinician can read in about a minute.

⚠️ FibroFog is a tracking tool, not a medical device, and nothing in it is a diagnosis or medical advice. Talk to your clinician before changing any treatment. Full disclaimer

Built for brain fog

Minimum effort on bad days. Data a doctor can actually use.

🔋

Low Energy Mode

Three giant buttons: Rough, Meh, Okay. One tap and today is logged. Big "Talk" and "Meds taken" buttons sit right below. Large text, high contrast, and oversized tap targets throughout, because a bad day is exactly when you need the log most. Gentle daily reminders skip themselves once you've logged.

🎙️

Just talk

Say "pain's about a seven, lower back and shoulders, really foggy today" and FibroFog fills in the log for you. The parser runs on your device and is careful by design: it fills what it's sure of and leaves the rest for you to confirm.

🌦️

Logs itself

Barometric pressure (and how much it changed since yesterday), humidity, and temperature are recorded automatically. Steps, sleep, workouts, resting heart rate, and HRV come from Apple Health. You don't lift a finger for any of it.

🧍

A body map that scores itself

Tap or swipe across the front and back figure to paint where it hurts, in mild, moderate, or severe. Your Widespread Pain Index and Symptom Severity Score (ACR 2016 criteria) update live. A list mode works fully with VoiceOver.

⛈️

Flares and what came before

FibroFog spots stretches well above your typical pain and looks back at what preceded them: very active days, long workouts, short sleep, pressure drops. The flare outlook reads the pressure forecast and your recent activity and gives you a heads-up when a big drop or an unusually busy week is coming.

🔗

Patterns, including delayed ones

Fibro rarely punishes you the same day. The pattern engine checks for effects up to three days later, so "more sleep tends to mean less brain fog the next day" can actually surface. Always labeled as associations, never as proof of cause. Alongside it, your baseline shows what "normal" looks like for you and what's different on your good days.

💊

Medications with a history

Track doses, check off what you've taken, and note changes and side effects as they happen. Past medications are kept, so your report shows what was tried and when.

📄

One page for your doctor

Pick 30 days, 90 days, or 6 months and get a single-page PDF: pain and symptom scores, medication adherence against pain, sleep and activity trends, flares, and the patterns FibroFog found. Print it or share it straight from the app.

🔐

Yours, and only yours

There is no FibroFog server and no account. Your log lives on your device and backs up to your own private iCloud. Weather lookups send only a rough location, rounded to about 10 km. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Under the hood

One codebase for iPhone today and Android soon.

🛠️

Tech stack

Built with Flutter and Dart. Data lives in on-device SQLite; the scoring, correlation, flare, and baseline logic is pure, unit-tested Dart. Doctor reports are rendered as PDFs on the device.

Android is on the way

FibroFog is coming to Android soon. It's the same app: steps and sleep from Health Connect, and backups to a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive instead of iCloud. Sync works between your iPhones and iPads, or between your Android devices.
