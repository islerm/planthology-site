⚠️ Important Disclaimer

FibroFog is a personal symptom tracker. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. The Widespread Pain Index and Symptom Severity Score follow the published ACR 2016 criteria but are self-reported tracking data, not a diagnosis. Flare outlooks, baselines, and patterns are statistical associations from your own entries; they are not proof of cause and may be wrong. Weather and health data come from third parties and your device and can be incomplete or inaccurate. Always talk to a qualified clinician before starting, stopping, or changing any medication or treatment. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

FAQ

What are WPI and SSS?

The Widespread Pain Index (0 to 19) counts the body regions where you've had pain; the Symptom Severity Score (0 to 12) rates fatigue, unrefreshing sleep, cognitive symptoms, and a few others. They come from the American College of Rheumatology's 2016 fibromyalgia criteria. FibroFog computes them from your log so you and your clinician can see how they move over time.

What is Low Energy Mode?

A stripped-down home screen with three huge buttons (Rough, Meh, Okay) plus Talk and Meds taken. One tap logs the day. Switch it on from the Today screen whenever a full log is too much.

How does voice logging work?

Tap Talk and describe your day. Your phone's built-in speech recognition turns it into text, then FibroFog's parser, which runs on your device, pulls out pain level, symptoms, and body areas. It only fills in what you actually said, and you review everything before saving.

How does it know the weather?

While you're using the app, FibroFog gets your approximate location, rounds it to about 10 km, and asks Open-Meteo for pressure, humidity, and temperature. It never tracks your location in the background.

Why does a pattern say "associations, not proof"?

FibroFog looks for things that tend to move together in your data, including effects up to three days later. That's useful for spotting possible triggers to discuss with your clinician, but two things moving together doesn't mean one causes the other.

How do I share the report with my doctor?

Open the Report tab, pick 30 days, 90 days, or 6 months, and tap Create report. From the preview you can print it or share the PDF by email, Messages, AirDrop, or a patient portal upload.

Where is my data stored? Does it sync?

On your device, with a backup in your own private iCloud. If you use FibroFog on more than one iPhone or iPad signed in to the same iCloud account, they sync. There is no FibroFog server, so I can't see or recover your data. See the privacy policy.

Is there an Android version?

It's coming soon. On Android, FibroFog will read steps and sleep from Health Connect and back up to a hidden, app-only folder in your own Google Drive. iCloud and Google Drive don't talk to each other, so data won't sync between an iPhone and an Android phone.
