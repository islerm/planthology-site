Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Your health is your business. FibroFog has no accounts and no FibroFog server, and I never receive your data. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. Your information is never sold or shared for advertising.

What stays on your device

Everything you log: pain levels, body map entries, symptoms, notes, medications and doses, voice memo transcripts, and report settings. It is stored in the app's local database on your device.

Backup and sync

On iPhone and iPad, FibroFog backs up and syncs through your own private iCloud account, in a container only FibroFog can use. On Android (coming soon), it will use the hidden, app-only folder in your own Google Drive; you choose whether to sign in, and FibroFog requests access only to that folder, not to the rest of your Drive. Apple and Google store this data under their own privacy policies.

Apple Health and Health Connect

With your permission, FibroFog reads steps, sleep, workouts, resting heart rate, and heart rate variability so it can relate activity and recovery to your symptoms. It never writes to Health. Health data is used only inside the app, is never used for advertising, and is never shared with anyone. You can revoke access at any time in your device's Health settings.

Location and weather

With your permission, and only while you're using the app, FibroFog reads your approximate location, rounds it to about 10 km, and sends just those rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, to get pressure, humidity, and temperature. No other information goes with the request. FibroFog never tracks your location in the background. Your last known location is kept on this device only, as a fallback for when location is unavailable; it is never synced or backed up to the cloud.

Microphone and speech

When you tap Talk, FibroFog uses your device's built-in speech recognition to turn your voice into text. Depending on your device and settings, Apple or Google may process that audio under their own privacy policies. FibroFog doesn't keep the audio; the resulting text is parsed on your device and saved only if you save the log.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Doctor reports

Reports are generated as PDFs on your device. They go only where you choose to print or share them.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any entry at any time, and turn off Health, location, microphone, or notification access in your device settings. Deleting the app deletes its local data; to remove the backup too, delete FibroFog's data from your iCloud (or Google Drive) storage settings.

Children

FibroFog is not directed at children under 13 and I collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue and I'll respond there.
